**The source advantage is modest and heterogeneous.** The component head has lower paper AUROC on **six of nine datasets**. Its largest gain is X-Fact **+6.703 pp**, followed by Companies **+2.696** and CounterFact **+1.245**; PopQA is **−1.021**. These are all retained in the fixed nine-dataset mean. The interval's positive lower endpoint is small and conditional on these datasets, not proof of broad superiority.

**Folded target gains do not imply better directed detection.** On target UHEAD, head versus logistic-14 directed AUROC is **39.885 versus 43.046**: the worse directed ranking becomes a **+3.161 pp folded gain**. WildHallucinations contributes **+3.930 pp** in both directions, while Common Claims loses **2.238 pp**. Consequently the target folded advantage coexists with an unresolved negative directed difference. Paper reporting folds each method/dataset using test-label-dependent orientation; it does not establish a deployable choice of score direction.

**Known claim overlap does not explain the small source-nine advantage.** Excluding every test row whose normalized claim occurs anywhere in PopQA train retains **96,961/97,488** source rows. Strict macro paper AUROC is **73.570 versus 72.949**, difference **+0.621 [0.040, 0.922]**; directed difference is **+0.751 [0.401, 1.127]**. On target/matched-source six, **56,228/56,235** rows remain. Target strict folded difference is **+0.814 [0.233, 1.370]**. This is normalized-claim-disjoint sensitivity, not entity/relation-disjoint evaluation or removal of the feature pool's selection history.

**Pairing was verified, not assumed.** For all nine source and six target datasets, the head and comparator have exact labels, masks, claim-group IDs and canonical NLL predictions; their dataset hashes, 512-row scoring chunks and claim-bootstrap settings agree. **All 500 saved NLL bootstrap values match exactly**, and the first five deterministic dataset-seeded claim draws were independently replayed from saved predictions. Full-sample CIs therefore use the paired saved head/comparator draws. Strict-subset CIs use 500 new shared claim draws from saved scores only, with the explicit seeds recorded in JSON. Folding precedes the fixed-dataset mean; a draw missing any prescribed dataset is excluded in full. All reported intervals have 500 usable draws.

**Fit and source provenance passed.** The grouped coefficient fit has **5,606 fit / 1,401 outer-validation rows**, no normalized-claim overlap, three grouped inner folds, a fold-local scaler, and five fixed C choices. **C=0.1** maximizes inner directed AUROC; no target refit occurs. Weight, wrapper, helper, fitter, pool, all fifteen component-program and parent-ablation hashes match their frozen records. The logistic-14 artifact is `b2f27a12db5177f004fa81f5df521f59436e4df2fcdc7f632e0eba847ecd4bce` and uses the same grouped partition, with its independently selected **C=100**. Head inner-CV / outer-validation AUROC is **81.544 / 82.094**, versus logistic-14 **82.346 / 82.167**; the head does not lead those diagnostics.

The feature pool was already selected using PopQA training/validation labels before this coefficient fit. Thus neither the new outer validation nor inner CV nests the **feature-discovery/selection process**: grouped splits and fit-only scaling prevent direct coefficient-fit leakage, but do not make those scores independent pipeline estimates. Benchmark outcomes had also been viewed before this follow-up was designed. It remains exploratory despite source-only coefficient fitting.

**Weights explain rebalancing, not causal importance.** Standardized coefficients include normalized slope **+0.662**, global correlation **+0.393**, diverse tail-gap variance **+0.470**, neutral tail-gap variance **+0.268**, weighted gap **−0.360**, and compositional tail variance **−0.351**. There are three almost identical NLL columns: pairwise fitting-set Pearson correlations exceed **0.999997**, each with coefficient approximately **+0.227**. Such redundancy makes individual weights unstable to representation and changes effective L2 regularization. A positive learned tail-mean coefficient does not contradict the fixed-formula ablation: its scale, surrounding features and other coefficients differ. Gains cannot be attributed solely to a novel signal or to complexity.

**73.633 versus the paper's quoted Evo-Anth-1 73.4 is numerical context only** (about +0.23 pp). This head adds supervised PopQA coefficient fitting, a validation-selected feature-discovery pool and different search/selection costs; the historical Claude implementation/predictions are unavailable for a paired comparison. It is not a matched Claude replication or a SOTA claim. Claim-bootstrap intervals condition on fixed programs, weights and datasets; they exclude search-seed/selection uncertainty and have no multiplicity correction.
