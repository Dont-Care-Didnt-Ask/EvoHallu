Duplicate-disjoint target-nine folded scores are **68.731 component head /64.781 NLL**. This removes exact normalized-claim overlap with the full PopQA training CSV; it does not establish entity-, relation- or semantic-disjoint generalization. The component pool was already source-validation-informed, coefficient CV did not nest feature discovery, and previous benchmark scores had been viewed. All findings remain exploratory. The 500 paired per-dataset claim draws quantify uncertainty conditional on frozen heads and this benchmark, not feature-discovery or writer-seed uncertainty. Per-dataset folding reproduces the paper's orientation convention and uses evaluation labels; it is not deployment calibration.

The separate main transfer cohort retains **30 method/run identities**: 27 source-validation primaries, two conventional fitted heads and canonical NLL. **29 have complete nine-dataset transfer; one remains eight-of-nine.** The additional component head is separate, giving 31 reported identities overall, rather than 31 distinct algorithms or 31 fully valid target methods.
